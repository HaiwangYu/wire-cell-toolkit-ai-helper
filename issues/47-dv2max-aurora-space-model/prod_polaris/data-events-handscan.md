**Columns**
- **hand-scan vertex.** The reference the distances use:
  - *scanner*: the "(x, y, z)" the scanner wrote for a Not OK / Not good round-2 vertex (eyeballed in Bee, ~1 cm);
  - *Xin r2 (scan OK)*: Xin's round-2 vertex, for events whose round-2 vertex was scanned Good/OK;
  - —: none (Not good without coordinates, or not scanned).
- **Xin r2 Bee.** Xin's round-2 Bee event, labelled with the scan verdict of its vertex.
- **`original` / `aurora` Bee.** Our two sets (same build, `e426d988` tree; same step-1 tars). Both sets use the same event order, so the index is the same in both.
- **summary.**
  - The distance to the hand-scan vertex (**bold** if < 1 cm; left out when our candidate is a different interaction);
  - the selection: νe = `nue_score > 7`, νμ = `numu_score > 0.9`, generic = `numu_cc_flag ≥ 0`;
  - the scores;
  - "trad. vertex" when the DL vertex was not taken.
- **notes.**
  - selection changes;
  - candidate mismatch;
  - the distance between our two vertices;
  - which arm is closer (> 1 cm better);
  - the scanner's comment on Xin's round 2 (it is about his round-2 reconstruction, not ours).

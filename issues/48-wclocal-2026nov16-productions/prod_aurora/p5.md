## Cost: the P4 estimate was too low

My P4 estimate (90–110 node-h) counted only process wall time. It left out the per-unit overhead.

Each unit makes about 8 SL7 container launches: step 2 ×2, collect ×2, step 3 ×2, validate ×2. Each launch costs about 25–30 s before work starts. Example, nueCC unit `10cc-3274-004a-8d4d` (4 events):
- process wall time: 211 s;
- elapsed: 447 s.

This overhead dominates the small nueCC (4.4 events/unit) and NC (1.5 events/unit) units.

Concurrency is also capped at **77 units per node** by the cgroup: pids.max 8192, with `WC_TASKS_PER_UNIT` = 100.
- BNB used **64 tasks per unit** on average, 72 at most.
- Load was about 75 on 208 hardware threads.
